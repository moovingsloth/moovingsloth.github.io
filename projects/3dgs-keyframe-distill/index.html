3DGS Keyframe Distillation

3D Gaussian Splatting (gsplat) on TUM RGB-D sequences.

A sharpness-and-coverage keyframe selection for 3D Gaussian Splatting: a modest gain over uniform sampling from a small share of the frames, reported with its limits.

  • Code on GitHub

On this page

Overview

3D Gaussian Splatting trains on posed images, and a handheld RGB-D sequence contains many blurry or near-duplicate frames. I designed a keyframe selection that scores sharpness and trajectory coverage together, and compared it with uniform sampling at budgets of 30 and 60 frames.

Setup

Renderer
3D Gaussian Splatting with gsplat, 7k iterations
Data
Three TUM RGB-D sequences: freiburg1_desk, freiburg1_room, freiburg2_xyz
Budgets
30 and 60 training frames, which is 1.7–21.1% of the available frames
Baselines
Uniform sampling, random, blur-only selection, and all frames
Seeds
Two per setting

Results

Across the six scene and budget settings, the selection averaged +0.55 dB PSNR (range 0.0–1.5) and +0.015 SSIM (0.004–0.025) over uniform sampling.

Limits

  • Results varied by scene. On desk at 60 frames, the PSNR difference was within seed noise.
  • Blur-only selection outperformed this method on fr2_xyz.
  • LPIPS was not measured.
